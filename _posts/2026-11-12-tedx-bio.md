I’m delighted to share my TEDxKalundborg talk, **“The Symphony of Biology,”** now available on the TEDx Talks YouTube channel.
In the talk, I explore biology as an orchestra, where understanding individual genes, proteins, and cells is only part of understanding the music of life.
The deeper insight comes from how these parts interact and work together.
I challenge the assumption that more data automatically leads to better decisions, highlighting why connecting biological evidence matters for how we think about medicine and healthcare.
Thank you to **TEDxKalundborg** for the opportunity to share this idea, and to **TEDx Talks** for making the recording available on their channel.
To my **family, friends, and everyone in the audience**, thank you for your support, encouragement, and curiosity.
I invite you to [watch “The Symphony of Biology” on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uwjbp6pkvG0) and reflect on what we can learn by listening to biology’s whole symphony.
